What is next?

  1. I filled up the form, what is next?
    • we will contact you within our schedule;
    • you can check our schedule on the Contacts page.
  2. I don't want to wait, what do i do?
    • our workers usually deal with the orders within 3 days after placing an order;
    • if 3 days already gone, you can call us to be updated on you order's status;
    • you can check how to reach us on the Contacts page.
  3. How do I reach you?
    • use semi-transparent menu in the right upper corner of the page;
    • if you use mobile version of the website, use the menu at the very top of the page;
    • click on Contacts option;
    • contacts page will provide you will all the information you need to reach us.
